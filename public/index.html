    VI
    

      
The fine print.

      
Short answers to the usual questions.

    

  

  

    
What exactly happens at the threshold?
When your coin's market cap crosses the value you set, its identity changes: image, name, ticker and description are replaced with the reveal you chose, or with a random meme from the TrojanCoins archive. Holders keep their tokens.

    
Can I change the threshold after launching?
No. The gate is set when you launch and is part of the coin's promise. Choose it carefully.

    
What does it cost to launch?
Creating a coin on pump.fun costs a small amount of SOL plus network fees, and you may add an optional initial buy. Phantom shows the exact total before you sign.

    
Who chooses the new meme?
You do, if you want to. When you launch, pick Surprise me and a random meme is drawn from the archive at the moment of reveal, or pick I choose and set the exact name, ticker, image and description the coin becomes.

    
Which wallets work?
Phantom, on Solana mainnet. Install the extension or the mobile app, then press Connect Phantom.

    
Is this financial advice?
No. Meme coins are volatile and can go to zero. Only use funds you can afford to lose.
